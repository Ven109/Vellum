A quiet place to write, on your own server.

Vellum is an open-source app for long-form writing. Run it yourself with one command, write offline on your laptop, and bring your own AI key. You pay your AI provider directly, and nobody charges you per seat.

Yours, in every sense

Host it yourself

One Docker Compose file runs the whole thing: sync, accounts, sharing and image storage. Your server, your data, and every feature included. Nothing is held back for a paid plan.

  • One-command setup with sensible defaults
  • Invite your team: roles, sharing links, comments and suggestions
  • Export everything as Markdown whenever you like

Bring your own AI key

Connect Anthropic, OpenAI, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint or a local model through Ollama. Your key stays on your device, and requests go straight to your provider.

  • Rewrites arrive as proposals: accept, try again or discard
  • House rules keep every suggestion in your voice
  • No AI at all? Vellum is a complete writing app without it

Made for long-form

A calm, serif-first editor with focus mode, an outline, version history you can compare and restore, and daily goals that stay on your device.

  • Works offline and syncs when you're back
  • Desktop apps for macOS, Windows and Linux
  • Import from Markdown, Notion and Google Docs

What about AI costs?

We don't sell AI. Rewrites and the assistant use your own API key, and your provider bills you directly for what you use. We never see your key, your prompts or your bill. With a local model through Ollama, it costs nothing at all.
